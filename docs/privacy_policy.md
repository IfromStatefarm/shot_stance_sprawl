# Snap & Go Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 24, 2026  
**Last updated:** October 1, 2026

Keep Kids Wrestling ("Keep Kids Wrestling," "we," "us," or "our") provides
the Snap & Go wrestling-training application. This policy explains what
information Snap & Go processes, why we process it, how it is protected, and
the choices available to you.

Snap & Go can be used for local workouts without creating an account. Account,
team, sharing, cloud progress, push-notification, and subscription features
require or are associated with a signed-in account.

## Information we process

### Account and profile information

When you create or connect an account, we process an account identifier and,
depending on the sign-in method you choose, your name, email address, profile
photo, and authentication provider. We also store the
display name, username, language, profile settings, and team membership you
choose. Authentication credentials are processed by Firebase Authentication
and the selected sign-in provider; we do not store a readable copy of your
password in Snap & Go's application database.

Snap & Go does not request access to your phone contacts. Users connect through
team access codes instead of address-book matching.

### Teams, photos, messages, and social activity

If you use social features, we process your team name, selected U.S. state,
team photo, membership and access information, friend or teammate
relationships, optional messages attached to shared workouts, reactions,
notification state, and timestamps for actions such as sending, accepting,
scheduling, starting, declining, or completing a shared workout. Your display
name, username, profile photo, and language may be visible to connected friends
or current teammates. Team details and the team photo are visible to current
team members. A shared workout and its message are visible to its sender and
recipient.

### Workout and fitness activity

Workout configuration, schedules, completion summaries, streaks, badges,
scores, and other training progress are fitness information. This information
normally stays on your device. When you sign in and link progress, join a team,
or send or complete a shared workout, the applicable workout and fitness data
is stored with your account so the requested cloud and social features work.

### Device and notification information

For a signed-in account, Snap & Go may process a random app-installation record,
Firebase installation identifier, push-notification token, platform, and last
active time to deliver and maintain notifications. Firebase also processes IP
addresses, user-agent information, and App Check attestation material for
authentication, service delivery, abuse prevention, and app integrity.

### Purchases

If you buy or restore Snap & Go Pro, we process the product identifier, store
provider, purchase or transaction identifier, purchase token or receipt,
subscription status, expiration date, and renewal state. We use this
information to validate the purchase, prevent one purchase from being attached
to multiple accounts, and provide the subscription. Apple or Google processes
your payment method; we do not receive or store your full payment-card or bank
account details.

### Camera, microphone, and local files

The app can record workout videos and custom audio callouts. Those recordings
and other local workout settings remain on your device unless you use your
device's sharing or export controls. Snap & Go does not upload workout videos
or custom audio recordings to our servers. A team photo is uploaded only when
you choose it for a team. Files already saved to your gallery, another app, or
an export location are controlled through that location.

## How we use information

We use the information above to:

- create, authenticate, secure, and administer accounts;
- provide profiles, teams, workout sharing, progress,
  subscriptions, and notifications;
- show requested information to teammates, workout recipients, and senders;
- verify purchases, prevent fraud and abuse, rate-limit sharing, troubleshoot
  failures, and maintain service integrity;
- provide data export and account-deletion tools; and
- comply with law and protect users, the public, and our rights.

We do not use Snap & Go information for third-party advertising, sell it, or
use it to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. Snap & Go
does not include an advertising or general-purpose analytics SDK.

## When information is disclosed

We disclose information only as needed for the following purposes:

- **People you choose to interact with.** Connected users and current
  teammates receive the profile, team, message, and workout information needed
  for the social action you request.
- **Service providers.** Google and Firebase provide authentication, cloud
  functions, database, storage, app-integrity, and messaging services. Apple
  and Google provide sign-in and in-app purchase services. These providers
  process information under their applicable terms and privacy notices.
- **Your direction.** The operating-system share sheet, SMS composer, gallery,
  or another destination receives information only when you direct the app to
  send or save it there.
- **Safety, law, and organizational changes.** We may disclose information when
  reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, address
  fraud or abuse, or complete a merger, financing, reorganization, or transfer
  of the service subject to appropriate safeguards.

## Retention and deletion

We use the following retention rules:

- Account, profile, cloud progress, device, social, workout-share, and
  developer-held purchase records are kept while the account is
  active, unless you delete them sooner through an available feature.
- A team photo is kept until it is replaced, the team is deleted, or the owner
  account is deleted. Temporary photo uploads are ordinarily removed after
  processing or a failed team operation; remaining uploads are removed when
  the account is deleted.
- Security, authentication, request, and rate-limit records are kept as needed
  to operate and protect the service and are removed with account data where
  they are account-linked. Firebase Authentication generally retains logged IP
  addresses for a few weeks.
- When you delete your account, Snap & Go deletes the Firebase Authentication
  user and associated developer-controlled profile, cloud progress, device,
  team, friendship, workout-share, social-event, social-stat, and
  social-qualification, partner-week, crew-progress, user-report, and purchase
  records. Deletion from Firebase live and backup systems may take up to 180
  days. We may retain a record longer if required by law, to resolve a dispute,
  or to prevent fraud, and Apple or Google may retain their own store records
  under their policies.
- Local recordings, exports, and app data on your device are not removed from
  other apps or locations by cloud account deletion. Delete those files from
  the destination where they are stored; uninstalling the app removes its
  remaining app-local data subject to your device backup settings.

You can export account data or delete your account in **Settings > Account**.
Deletion requires a recent sign-in. If you no longer have the app, you can
[request account deletion on the web](https://keepkidswrestling.com/Snap-and-go/account-deletion)
or email us for help with access, correction, export, or deletion. We may need
to verify the request before acting on it. Deleting a Snap & Go account does
not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription; cancel an active
subscription through the applicable store to prevent future billing.

## Security and encryption

Connections to Firebase use HTTPS encryption in transit. Firebase states that
the Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Cloud
Storage, and App Check services used by Snap & Go encrypt data at rest. Access
is further limited with authentication, security rules, trusted server
functions, and App Check. Local data is protected by the security controls of
your device. No system is completely secure, and Snap & Go messages and shared
workouts are not end-to-end encrypted.

## Children's privacy

Snap & Go is intended for users age 13 and older. Before onboarding, the app
asks the user to select either **Under 13** or **13 or older**. This age-range
answer is stored on the device and is used to control feature availability; we
do not upload the answer to our servers.

An under-13 selection places the app in local training mode. Accounts, teams,
messaging, social and cloud sharing, connected push
notifications, and purchases are unavailable, and the app does not initialize
those connected services for that path. We do not provide child accounts,
verified parental consent, or parental management of child social features.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. If you
believe a child under 13 supplied personal information, contact us and we will
take steps to delete it. Parents and guardians may contact us about a minor's
information.

The local, no-account workout experience does not require personal information
to be sent to us. Profile photos, custom audio, review videos, workout settings,
and progress created in that mode remain on the device unless the user exports
them using operating-system controls.

## International processing

Firebase Authentication is operated from the United States. Other Firebase
services may process information on Google infrastructure in the United States
and other countries. Privacy protections in those countries may differ from
those where you live.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or legal requirements change. We
will update the date above and provide additional notice when appropriate.
