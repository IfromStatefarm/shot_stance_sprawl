Delete your Snap & Go account

You can request permanent deletion here even if you no longer have the app. We will verify that you own the account before deleting it and its associated data.

Send a deletion request

If possible, email us from the address connected to your Snap & Go account. Include your username and sign-in method so we can locate and verify the account. Never send us your password or a verification code.

Request deletion by email

If the button does not open your email app, write to info@keepkidswrestling.com with the subject “Snap & Go account deletion request.”

You can also delete in the app

Sign in, then open Settings → Account → Delete account. In-app deletion requires a recent sign-in and starts immediately after confirmation.

What deletion covers

After verification, we delete the account and associated developer-controlled data, including:

  • authentication, profile, username, device, and contact-lookup records;
  • cloud progress, team membership or owned team, photos, friendships, and workout shares;
  • social events, statistics, qualification records, crew progress, and user reports; and
  • developer-held subscription and purchase-verification records.

Subscriptions and data kept elsewhere

Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel an active subscription in your store account to prevent future billing. You can request account deletion before or after canceling.

Local recordings, exported files, and copies saved to other apps remain where you saved them. Delete those copies separately. Apple, Google, and other service providers may retain their own transaction or security records under their policies.

Timing and limited retention

We process verified requests within a reasonably quick period. Deletion from Firebase live and backup systems may take up to 180 days. We may retain limited records longer when required by law, needed to resolve a dispute, or necessary to prevent fraud or abuse.

For more detail, read the Snap & Go Privacy Policy.
