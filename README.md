# shot_stance_sprawl

Shadow wrestling drill assistant. Firebase environment setup is documented in
[`docs/firebase_environments.md`](docs/firebase_environments.md); Apple signing,
CocoaPods, archiving, and TestFlight validation are documented in
[`docs/ios_release.md`](docs/ios_release.md). The public-facing privacy policy
source and the App Store/Google Play disclosure checklist are maintained in
[`docs/privacy_policy.md`](docs/privacy_policy.md) and
[`docs/store_privacy_disclosures.md`](docs/store_privacy_disclosures.md). The
standalone Google Play account-deletion request resource is
[`web/account-deletion/index.html`](web/account-deletion/index.html) and must be
published at
`https://keepkidswrestling.com/Snap-and-go/account-deletion`.
